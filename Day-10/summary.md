# Day 10 – Summary

Today I learned how Publisher and Subscriber nodes communicate in ROS 2 through a topic.

I created a Python Publisher and Subscriber using `rclpy`. The Publisher sent String messages to `/chat_topic`, and the Subscriber successfully received them.

I also used ROS 2 commands to check nodes, topics, topic information, message types, and published messages.

This practical helped me understand asynchronous communication between ROS 2 nodes and the role of topics, messages, callbacks, and QoS.
